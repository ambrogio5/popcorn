## Description

Popcorn is a bubble-popping arcade game in the spirit of the 1989 classic Pang: shoot a line
straight up, split the bouncing bubbles until they pop, climb ladders, and don't get touched.

Everything is open from the first launch. Sixty-four levels across eight cities, an endless Storm
mode, and two-player co-op on one screen. There are no ads, no coins, no timers, no energy, no
loot boxes and nothing to buy. The only thing you unlock is skill.

Built for iPhone Duo: open the phone and the arena widens into a 16 × 12 field with room for two
players, one in each corner. Close it and the game reshapes into a tall single-player arena without
missing a beat. Works on every iPhone running iOS 18 or later.

- 64 hand-tuned levels, all unlocked
- Six bubble types: plain, armored, lightning, flipped, triple and golden
- Five power-ups that drop from bubbles: double shot, sticky line, rapid fire, shield, freeze
- Combos up to ×8 with a rising pop scale
- Getting hit never resets a level — the room freezes, you get back up, the checkpoint is wherever you were
- Two players, one screen (best on iPhone Duo or iPad)
- Game-controller and keyboard support
- Three stars per level: finish, no hits, beat par

## Privacy (App Privacy section)

**Data not collected.** Popcorn has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no network access
and no third-party SDKs. Progress and settings are stored in `UserDefaults` on the device and go
no further. If Game Center is enabled, scores are submitted through Apple's GameKit under Apple's
own privacy policy; the app itself still collects nothing.
